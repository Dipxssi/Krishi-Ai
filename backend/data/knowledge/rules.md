# Fertilizer and safety rules

## Nitrogen caution
Do not recommend high nitrogen doses during active fungal outbreaks on leafy crops. Excess vegetative growth worsens disease microclimates.

## Pesticide safety
Never invent pesticide brand names or doses without a retrieved source. If evidence is missing, escalate to an agronomist and recommend non-chemical cultural practices first.

## Evidence rule
Numeric claims about price, rainfall, cost, and profit must come from tools. Forecasts must be labeled with horizon and uncertainty.
